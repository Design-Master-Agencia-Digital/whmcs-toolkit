# WHMCS Toolkit - Privacy Policy

Version 1, effective November 4, 2026.

WHMCS Toolkit is a plugin for JetBrains IDEs published by A De Bortolo Soluções em Internet Ltda (trading as Design Master), CNPJ 10.620.937/0001-13, Rua Cassiano Castelo, 276, sala 301, Centro, Colatina/ES, 29700-060, Brazil.

## What the plugin collects

Nothing. The plugin runs entirely inside your IDE. It reads the PHP files of the project you open only to offer completion, inspections, quick documentation and quick fixes, and it keeps that analysis in memory on your machine.

The plugin does not open network connections, does not send telemetry, analytics or crash reports to us, and does not store your code, project names, credentials or WHMCS data anywhere outside your IDE.

## Licensing and payments

Purchases, trials and license checks are handled by JetBrains s.r.o. through JetBrains Marketplace and the IDE's built-in licensing. We do not receive your payment details. JetBrains processes that data under its own privacy policy: https://www.jetbrains.com/legal/docs/privacy/privacy/

## Crash and freeze reports

If you choose to send an exception or freeze report from the IDE, it goes to JetBrains Marketplace, which shows it to us so we can fix the bug. Those reports are sent only when you submit them, and they are covered by the JetBrains privacy policy above.
